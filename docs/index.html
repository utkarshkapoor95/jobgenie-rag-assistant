What makes it different

Most career tools are a search box or a chatbot, not both. JobGenie is the one that is both: it searches real job boards for you, then lets you ask it anything about your results, your resume, or your career, in the same conversation.

●

Real jobs, not a demo dataset

Every listing is pulled live from JSearch, which aggregates Google for Jobs' index, LinkedIn, Indeed, company career pages and dozens of smaller boards. Click "Apply" and you land on the actual listing.

●

Ask it anything about your career

Not just a search tool: ask about salary expectations, how to frame a career switch, which of two job descriptions fits better, or what to say in an interview, grounded in your actual resume.

01

Resume becomes searchable

The PDF is parsed, split into chunks, and embedded with a sentence-transformer model into Pinecone, a vector database. This is what lets later questions ("what's my fit for this JD") retrieve the relevant parts of the resume instead of guessing from the whole document at once.

02

An LLM call suggests titles

A separate Groq call (openai/gpt-oss-120b) reads the embedded resume and proposes three job titles likely to return real search results, not just ones that sound impressive. One click starts the search.

03

Live listings get pulled and scored

JSearch queries Google for Jobs' index, which aggregates from LinkedIn, Indeed, company career pages and dozens of smaller boards in one call. Each listing is then scored against the resume by a second LLM call: matched skills, missing skills, and a fit percentage, not a keyword match.

04

Your history is remembered

Search results and application status are saved to a small persistent store keyed to your email. Come back later with the same email and JobGenie tells you what's new since your last visit, something a one-off chat session can't do.

What it tracks for you

The two below are the difference between JobGenie and pasting your resume into a chat: a chat forgets you the moment you close it.

Returning-user memory

Come back with the same email and see what's changed since your last search, new listings are flagged automatically. No chat session can do this, it has no memory once you close it.

Application tracker

Mark a job as applied in one click. Your status is saved and carried forward even if that listing shows up again in a later search, weeks from now.

Skill gap aggregation

Instead of showing gaps per job, JobGenie rolls them up across every result so you can see what to learn first.

Compare job descriptions

Paste up to three JDs side by side and get a single recommendation on which one fits your profile best.
