How it works
01

Upload your resume

JobGenie reads it, embeds it into a vector index, and suggests three job titles worth searching, picked from what's actually on the page.

02

Pick a title and location

One click on a suggested title, a country, and a city. No long forms, no typing a search query from scratch.

03

Get a scored shortlist

Live listings come back ranked by fit, with matched and missing skills called out, plus links to learn whatever's missing.

What it tracks for you

Returning-user memory

Come back with the same email and see what's changed since your last search, new listings are flagged automatically.

Application tracker

Mark a job as applied in one click. Your status is saved and carried forward even if that listing shows up again in a later search.

Skill gap aggregation

Instead of showing gaps per job, JobGenie rolls them up across every result so you can see what to learn first.

Compare job descriptions

Paste up to three JDs side by side and get a single recommendation on which one fits your profile best.
